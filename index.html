Questions

Before you call

If your question isn't here, call or text and you'll get a straight answer.

Call or text 410-357-1406
Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We hold a Maryland Home Improvement Commission license (MHIC # pending) and Pennsylvania Home Improvement Contractor registration (PA HIC # pending), and we carry full insurance. Every operator and mason is trained to OSHA standards.

Is the quote free?

Yes. We visit the site, take measurements and send an itemized estimate with no obligation.

Do you supply the hoist?

No. We supply trained operators for your hoist, inside or outside. If you need help finding equipment, our procurement team can source it.

Can you match my existing brick or stone?

Usually, yes. Our suppliers carry hard-to-find brick and stone, including matches for older rowhomes and historic buildings.

Which cleaning phases do you offer?

Rough clean during construction, final clean before turnover and touch-up clean before move-in or inspection. Ask for a custom scope if your job needs something else.

Do you work with homeowners or only contractors?

Both. We work with homeowners, landlords, property managers, general contractors and developers, on jobs from a single stoop to a multi-story build.
