Why we started Avant

The team we wished we'd had

We've been on the other side of the contract: waiting on callbacks, watching deadlines slip, hiring someone who said the right things and didn't follow through.

Avant exists to be the crew we always wanted on our own jobs. That means showing up when we said we would, answering the phone, pricing the work line by line and leaving the site clean.

Lead operator

The person on your site

Jimmy Harris has run hoists as a Local 542 operator and laid brick with BAC Local 1. He trains every operator we place and walks every masonry job before it's quoted.

That pairing is why a hoist call and a facade repair get the same attention. He knows what rides on every load because he's set the brick at the top of it.
