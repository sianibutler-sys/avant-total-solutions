Privacy policy

Effective October 20, 2026

This policy explains what information Avant Total Solutions collects through avanttotalsolutions.com and how we use it.

What we collect

When you send the quote form, we receive what you enter: the services you picked, your job ZIP code, your timeline, your description of the work, and your name, phone number and email. When you call, text or email us, we receive what you send.

Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, to deliver and secure the site.

How we use it

We use your information to reply to you, prepare quotes, schedule and perform work, and keep records of jobs we do. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the services that run our business: our website host, our email provider, and the tools we use to receive and track quote requests. They process it on our behalf. We may also share information when the law requires it.

Cookies and third parties

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads. Links to LinkedIn and Instagram take you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

Photos of our work

Project photos on this site show our own work. We remove location data from every photo before we publish it. If you see a photo of your property and want it removed, email us and we'll take it down.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep quote requests and job records as long as we need them for the work, our warranty and our legal and tax obligations. To see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, email hello@avanttotalsolutions.com.

Children

This site is meant for adults arranging construction work. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
