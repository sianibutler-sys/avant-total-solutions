What can you source?

Building materials like brick, block, CMU, stone, lumber, drywall and concrete products, plus equipment rentals, fixtures, finishes and furnishings.

Do you have preferred suppliers?

Yes. We work with suppliers we know for consistent quality, fair pricing and on-time delivery. If you have a brand or supplier in mind, we can work with them too.

Can you handle rush orders?

Whenever possible, yes. If you need something fast, we find the best available option and get it to your site as soon as we can.

Do you work with homeowners?

Case by case. Most of our procurement work supports contractors and multi-unit projects, and we're glad to talk about large renovations or specialty finds.

How do I start?

Call, text or send the form with what you need, quantities and your delivery date. We confirm specs and budget before we source anything.
